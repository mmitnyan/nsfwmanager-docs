# End-User Licence Agreement (EULA)
## Viewing the Terms That Govern Your Use of NSFW Manager

The EULA dialog displays the complete End-User Licence Agreement for NSFW Manager. Open it from **Help → EULA** in the menu bar.

---

## What the EULA Covers

The agreement defines your rights and restrictions as a user of NSFW Manager. It covers:

- **Permitted uses** — what you may do with the software on your own files
- **Restrictions** — what you may not do (redistribution, reverse engineering, bypassing licence controls)
- **Intellectual property** — ownership of the software and bundled AI models
- **Anonymous telemetry disclosure** — what is sent to our server during trial/unlicensed use, and how it stops once a paid licence is activated (Section 9)
- **Liability disclaimer** — limitations on warranty and damages
- **Governing law** — the jurisdiction applicable to disputes

The EULA is a legally binding agreement between you and Manuel Mitnyan (copyright © 2024-2026). By installing and using NSFW Manager, you agree to its terms.

---

## Three Languages, One Dialog

The EULA dialog presents the agreement in three tabs:

| Tab | Language |
|---|---|
| English | Full agreement in English |
| Français | Full agreement in French |
| Español | Full agreement in Spanish |

All three versions are equivalent in content. The English version is the authoritative reference in case of interpretation conflicts.

The dialog is resizable and scrollable — use the scroll bar on the right to navigate the full text.

---

## EULA File in the Installation Directory

The full agreement text is also available as a standalone file at:

```
%LOCALAPPDATA%\NsfwManager\licenses\EULA.txt
```

This file is installed alongside the application and can be read with any text editor. It serves as the permanent offline record of the agreement you accepted at install time.
